# PetCare Site Vision & Roadmap

## 1. Site Vision
PetCare is a modern, action-oriented e-commerce platform for pet supplies. The goal is to maximize sales and user engagement with a dynamic, clean, and accessible UI, prioritizing high-quality product displays and straightforward navigation.

## 2. Target Audience
Pet owners looking for a convenient, premium shopping experience for dog, cat, and other pet supplies.

## 3. Core Features
- Action-first hero section
- Easy-to-use category navigation
- Comprehensive product listings with quick add-to-cart functionality
- Best seller and promotional showcases
