A high-converting Login and Registration page for PetCare.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **Theme:** Light mode
- **Primary Color:** #F4D35E
- **Typography:** Inter (or similar modern sans-serif)
- **Corner Style:** Rounded (friendly, approachable)
- **Aesthetic:** Modern, clean, "action-first" e-commerce. High contrast, vibrant hero sections, equal-height product cards with 1:1 images, subtle hover effects (scale + shadow).

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header:** 
   - Logo left: "PetCare 🐾"
   - Minimal header to reduce distraction, no main menu navigation. Just a "Back to Shop" text link.
2. **Main Layout (Split Screen):**
   - **Left Column (Visual/Brand):** Large, beautiful 1:1 lifestyle image of a happy dog and cat. Overlay text with `display-md`: "Welcome Back to PetCare." and `body-lg`: "Your pets miss you. Log in to track orders, manage subscriptions, and earn rewards."
   - **Right Column (Forms):** A clean, white `surface-container-lowest` card with subtle drop shadow.
     - Tabs for "Log In" and "Sign Up".
     - **Form Elements:** Input fields with `surface-container-low` background and floating labels. Fields: Email Address, Password. 
     - "Forgot Password?" text link.
     - Huge, vibrant #F4D35E primary CTA button: "Log In".
     - "Or continue with" section using Social Login buttons (Google, Apple, Facebook) styled as outlined pill buttons.
3. **Footer:**
   - Minimal footer. Just Copyright and Links to Privacy Policy / Terms.
