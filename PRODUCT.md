# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Pet owners who treat their animals as family members. They are looking for high-quality products, reliable veterinary advice, and premium grooming services in one place. They value trust, expertise, and a seamless shopping experience.

## Product Purpose
PetCare is a comprehensive ecosystem for pet wellness. It bridges the gap between e-commerce and professional healthcare, providing a single destination for everything from organic treats to expert surgical consultations.

## Brand Personality
Warm, Professional, Elevated.
The brand should feel as welcoming as a local pet shop but as competent as a top-tier medical facility. It avoids "cartoonish" pet clichés in favor of clean, modern, and trustworthy aesthetics.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, low-contrast legacy veterinary portals.
- Generic, high-saturation "Big Box" pet store templates.
- Overly playful or childish designs that undermine professional expertise.

## Design Principles
1. **Emotional Proximity**: Lead with high-quality imagery that evokes the bond between pets and humans.
2. **Expert Clarity**: Use typography and layout to establish hierarchy, making services feel authoritative and accessible.
3. **Seamless Transitions**: Ensure the journey from "browsing for fun" to "booking for health" feels cohesive and low-friction.
4. **Organic Precision**: Use soft shapes and warm tones balanced by precise grid systems and crisp typography.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.
- High color contrast for critical service information.
- Support for multiple languages (EN, VI, ZH) as established in the codebase.
